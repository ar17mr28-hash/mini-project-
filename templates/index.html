⚓ Underwater Debris & Containers

Hard man-made structures present intense acoustic backscatter reflections accompanied by clear acoustic shadow voids opposite the sonar tow-fish.

🕸️ Ghost Fishing Nets

Abandoned synthetic fishing nets snagged on reefs or sediments produce diffuse, high-entropy cluster boundaries and localized acoustic dispersion.

📏 Subsea Pipelines & Cables

Continuous elongated linear geometries identified through morphological aspect ratio analysis (high width-to-height structural ratios).

🚢 Sunken Hull Wreckage

Extensive multi-component metallic debris with extensive acoustic occlusion shadows, requiring multi-scale contour segmentation.
