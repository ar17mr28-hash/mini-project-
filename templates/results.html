Acoustic Analysis Methodology Explanation (Academic Review)

Physical Principle: Side-scan sonar transducers project fan-shaped acoustic pulses across the ocean floor. Hard protruding objects (such as ship hulls, container boxes, or metal pipes) strongly reflect sound waves back to the hydrophone, creating intense specular backscatter (bright highlights). Simultaneously, sound cannot penetrate these objects, leaving an acoustic shadow void (dark pixels) directly behind the object.

Processing Logic: AquaSentinel applies adaptive statistical thresholds relative to the mean seabed backscatter ($\mu \pm 1.8\sigma$). Gaussian smoothing and morphological kernels remove high-frequency acoustic speckle noise while preserving macroscopic target contours. Candidates exceeding $300\text{ px}$ are registered and framed in bounding boxes.
